Mini Tumbleweed

Place stacks on a tight hex board. A new stack's height equals the strongest visible friendly support nearby.

Rules

Choose an empty hex. Its stack height is one plus the highest adjacent friendly stack, capped at 5.

A placement is legal only when it touches friendly support or the opening center.

After 18 total placements, higher stack control around empty spaces decides the winner.
