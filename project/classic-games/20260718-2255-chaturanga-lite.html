Rules

Capture the rival raja or advance a soldier to the far rank.

Raja steps one square, ratha slides straight, ashva jumps in an L, gaja leaps two diagonals, and soldiers step forward or capture diagonally.

This compact 5x5 board keeps the old piece identities while making a fast browser-friendly duel.
